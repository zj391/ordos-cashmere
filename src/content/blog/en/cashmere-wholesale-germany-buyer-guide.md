# Cashmere Wholesale Germany: Direct Ordos Import Guide for German B2B Buyers

The German cashmere market imports approximately €120 million annually, primarily from Inner Mongolia and Mongolia. The majority flows through Hamburg, Frankfurt and Munich-based importers, who typically charge 25–40 % markup over factory price.

This guide shows German B2B buyers how to work directly with verified Ordos mills and save €30–80 per kg.

## Certifications for the EU market

German importers require these certifications:
- **OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class I** — safe for direct skin contact
- **REACH compliance** — EU chemical regulation
- **IWTO certification** — international wool standard
- **GB/T 21209** — Chinese national standard equivalent
- **GCS (Good Cashmere Standard)** — sustainability and traceability

## MOQ and delivery conditions

| Parameter | Standard |

## Quality verification before bulk orders

Three mandatory checks:
1. **OFDA fiber test** — diameter distribution (Grade A = <15.5 µm)
2. **Length test** — ≥38 mm staple fiber length
3. **GB/T 21209 compliance** — Chinese national standard

## Direct import vs. via Hamburg importer

Comparison for a 380 g sweater, Grade A cashmere, 1000 pieces:

| Source | Price per piece |
|---|---|
| **FOB Tianjin direct** | €48–62 |
| **Via Hamburg importer** | €78–110 |
| **Retail price** | €180–280 |

The €30–48 difference per piece between FOB and importer price equals accumulated intermediary margin. Direct import enables 35–50 % cost savings at the same quality.

## Logistics and shipping

For German importers:
- **FOB Tianjin/Shanghai** → Sea freight to Hamburg: 28–35 days
- **CIF Hamburg** — including insurance and freight
- **LCL shipping** for trial orders <500 kg
- **FCL shipping** (20′/40′ containers) for bulk orders

## Customs clearance

German importers need:
- **Commercial invoice** with HS code 5108 (cashmere)
- **Certificate of origin Form A** for EU preference
- **Declaration of conformity** for OEKO-TEX
- **Bill of lading**
