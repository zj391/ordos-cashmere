# 10 Types of Cashmere Yarn We Make at Our Ordos Facility: 2026 Yarn Formulation Guide

Not all cashmere yarn is created equal — and not all cashmere yarn projects need the same yarn count, fiber blend, or MOQ. At our Ordos facility, we spin ten distinct cashmere yarn formulations across four blend categories. Each formulation has been validated through full-scale production, with stable pricing for 2026 and reliable lead times for repeat orders.

## 1. Pure Cashmere 2/26NM (Stock Workhorse)

**Blend**: 100% Grade A Mongolian cashmere
**Use case**: Standard machine-knitting and weaving yarn for sweaters, scarves, accessories
**MOQ**: 50 kg per count | **FOB Tianjin**: USD 95–120 per kg

The 2/26NM 100% cashmere is our facility's workhorse stock yarn. It powers the majority of our sweater and scarf production, with 73 yarn configurations (color + count + blend variations) available from inventory. For buyers building classic cashmere knitwear, this is the default choice.

## 2. Pure Cashmere 2/36NM (Mid-Gauge Lightweight)

**Blend**: 100% Grade A Mongolian cashmere
**Use case**: Mid-gauge pure cashmere for lightweight knitwear and refined jersey
**MOQ**: 50 kg per count | **FOB Tianjin**: USD 105–135 per kg

The 2/36NM count is a step finer than the standard 2/26, used for lightweight knitwear, refined jersey tops, and thin scarves. The higher yarn count reduces fabric weight per square meter, producing garments that drape more cleanly and feel cooler against the skin — particularly valued for transitional-season retail collections.

## 3. Pure Cashmere 3/68NM (Fine Worsted)

**Blend**: 100% Grade A Mongolian cashmere
**Use case**: Fine-gauge worsted cashmere for premium dress weight and ultra-fine knit
**MOQ**: 50 kg per count | **FOB Tianjin**: USD 145–180 per kg

The 3/68NM is our finest pure cashmere yarn, used for premium dress-weight knitwear, ultra-fine jersey, and luxury lightweight layering pieces. The worsted spinning process produces a smoother, more refined yarn surface than the 2/26 or 2/36 — at a 50%+ premium over the standard count.

## 4. Pure Cashmere 1/4.3NM & 1/6.5NM (Chunky Weaving)

**Blend**: 100% Grade A Mongolian cashmere
**Use case**: Chunky-weaving and hand-knit yarn for blanket, throw, home textile
**MOQ**: 50 kg per count | **FOB Tianjin**: USD 85–110 per kg

Our chunky-count cashmere yarn — at 1/4.3NM and 1/6.5NM — is the heaviest weight in our pure-cashmere range. It is used primarily for hand-knit blankets, throws, and home-textile applications, where the yarn's bulk and warmth expression matter more than fabric density. Wholesale pricing per kg is lower than finer counts because the yarn count converts more raw fiber per kg.

## How to Choose the Right Formulation

Most private-label buyers fall into one of three selection patterns:

**Pattern A — Classic Cashmere Program** (the most common)
Default to **2/26NM 100% cashmere** for sweaters and scarves. Use **3/68NM** for premium-tier lightweight layering. Add **2/36NM** if you need a mid-weight jersey.

**Pattern B — Price-Tier Diversification** (retail brands with multiple price points)
Stock 100% cashmere (premium tier), cashmere-wool 30/70 (mid tier), and cashmere-linen 65/35 (transitional tier) across 3 SKUs.

**Pattern C — Signature Product Line** (boutique brands seeking differentiation)
Build your brand around one of the signature blends — YARK 50/50 or Taiji Stone 10/40/50. These yarns become brand-defining SKUs that competitors cannot easily replicate.
