# Cashmere Trade Shows 2026: The Complete Calendar for B2B Cashmere Buyers

If you source cashmere for a brand, fashion house, or retail chain, the trade show calendar shapes your buying cycle more than any other industry event. The right show connects you with mills, spinners, and garment makers who can deliver your AW26/SS27 collections on time. The wrong show burns three days and a USD 4,000 flight for nothing. This calendar covers the shows that actually matter for cashmere buyers in 2026, what each one is best for, and how to plan your visit.

We've attended 12+ cashmere-focused trade shows over the last decade, hosted booths at three of them, and walked the rest as visitors. The calendar below is the practical version — not a comprehensive industry directory, but the shows where you can see real cashmere products and talk to factory owners who can ship your order.

## The 2026 Cashmere Trade Show Calendar

Shows are organized chronologically. Most happen twice a year (February for SS collections, September-October for AW collections). Some smaller shows are annual.

### SpinExpo (Shanghai, NYC, Biella)

**Dates 2026**: SpinExpo Shanghai — March 4-6, 2026 (SS27 yarn preview). SpinExpo Shanghai — September 2-4, 2026 (AW27 yarn preview). SpinExpo New York — December 8-10, 2026 (specialty yarn showcase).

**Focus**: Yarn and fiber. SpinExpo is the most cashmere-focused yarn trade show globally. Most Chinese, Mongolian, Italian, and Scottish spinners attend. You'll see 2/26 Nm, 2/48 Nm, 100% cashmere, cashmere/silk blends, and cashmere/yak — across hundreds of mills.

**Why it matters for cashmere buyers**: If you buy yarn (rather than finished garments), SpinExpo Shanghai is essential. Two of our three yarn suppliers exhibit here annually — seeing their new collections in person lets you pre-select colors and feel yarn quality before committing to a yarn sample order. The March show is the right place to source SS27; the September show sets up AW27.

**Registration**: Free for trade buyers with business credentials (company name, tax ID, import license). Register 6-8 weeks ahead via spin-expo.com. Pre-registration gets you a buyer badge that waives the on-site registration fee (USD 50-100 otherwise).

### Première Vision (Paris, NYC)

**Dates 2026**: Première Vision Paris — February 10-12, 2026 (SS27). Première Vision Paris — September 15-17, 2026 (AW27). Première Vision New York — January 13-14, 2026 and July 14-15, 2026.

**Focus**: Premium fabric, yarn, and accessories across all fibers (wool, silk, cotton, synthetics, plus dedicated cashmere halls). Higher-end fashion houses, design studios, and brand sourcing teams attend.

**Why it matters for cashmere buyers**: PV Paris in September is where European luxury brands place AW orders for the following year. If you're a brand targeting premium retail (USD 200+ retail price points), PV is the place to be seen and to find European and Italian mills that supply into Chanel, Loro Piana, and Brunello Cucinelli. Italian mills showing here typically source their cashmere fiber from China or Mongolia — so the yarn quality is comparable to ours, but the finishing and brand reputation command a 30-50% premium.

**Registration**: Free for trade buyers, but PV uses a tiered invitation system. New buyers register via premierevision.com and request an invitation 4-6 weeks ahead. Premium buyers (those with USD 5M+ annual fiber spend) get automatic invites.

### Milano Unica (Milan)

**Dates 2026**: Milano Unica — February 4-6, 2026 (SS27). Milano Unica — July 14-16, 2026 (AW27).

**Focus**: Italian textile excellence — wool, cashmere, silk, linen, cotton. Heavily Italian mills (Loro Piana, Zegna Baruffa, Reda, Lanificio Cerruti, plus 400+ smaller mills).

**Why it matters for cashmere buyers**: Italian mills have a cachet that Asian mills don't. If your brand positioning leans "Made in Italy" or "Italian finishing", Milano Unica lets you meet the mills directly. Italian cashmere garments typically cost 50-80% more than Chinese-manufactured cashmere at the same fiber grade — partly justified by finishing and partly by the label.

**Registration**: Trade buyer registration via milanounica.it. Free with verified business credentials.

### Magic Trade Show (Las Vegas)

**Dates 2026**: Magic Las Vegas — February 9-11, 2026 (SS). Magic Las Vegas — August 17-19, 2026 (AW). Project (premium segment) co-located.

**Focus**: Finished apparel and accessories. US-market-focused. Lots of ready-to-wear cashmere sweaters, scarves, beanies from Chinese factories with US warehouses.

**Why it matters for cashmere buyers**: If you buy finished garments (not yarn), Magic is where US-based brands source the bulk of their inventory. The August edition is critical for Q4 holiday season — placing orders here in August for October-November delivery is a tight but workable cycle for stock programs.

**Registration**: USD 100-200 trade buyer pass. Register via magicfashionevents.com.

### Pure London / Pure Origin (London)

**Dates 2026**: Pure London — February 8-10, 2026 (SS27). Pure London — July 12-14, 2026 (AW27). Pure Origin co-located.

**Focus**: UK and European brands, designer collections, premium ready-to-wear. Smaller than PV but more accessible for new buyers.

**Why it matters for cashmere buyers**: Pure is where UK boutique brands and European design studios source. Less Asian factory presence than SpinExpo, but more design-led brands. If your customer base is UK/EU mid-market (USD 80-200 retail), Pure is worth a visit.

**Registration**: Free for verified trade buyers via purelondon.com.

### Inner Mongolia Cashmere Industry Expo (Ordos)

**Dates 2026**: Inner Mongolia Cashmere Industry Expo — typically held August 18-20, 2026 in Ordos (the cashmere capital of China). Date is sometimes adjusted by the local cashmere association — verify with the Ordos government commerce bureau 6 months ahead.

**Focus**: 100% Inner Mongolian cashmere. All major Ordos mills, spinners, and garment makers exhibit. Raw fiber, dehaired cashmere, yarn, fabric, finished garments — full vertical.

**Why it matters for cashmere buyers**: This is the only major trade show where you can compare 50+ Inner Mongolian cashmere mills in person. Most mills will arrange factory tours after the show — visitors typically spend 2-3 days at the expo then 2-3 days touring Ordos facilities. If your buying volume justifies a China trip (USD 50K+ annual), this show + tours is the most efficient way to evaluate 5-10 factories in one week.

**Registration**: Free or USD 50-100 depending on the year. Register via the Ordos commerce bureau or local industry associations.

### China Import & Export Fair (Canton Fair, Guangzhou)

**Dates 2026**: Canton Fair Phase 1 (industrial, electronics, textiles) — April 15-19, 2026. Canton Fair Phase 3 (apparel, textiles, fashion) — October 31 - November 4, 2026.

**Focus**: Mass-market export manufacturing. Cashmere is a small segment but exists — mostly in Phase 3.

**Why it matters for cashmere buyers**: If you need very high-volume cashmere (10,000+ pieces per SKU per color), Canton Fair has suppliers who specialize in scale. Quality is generally mid-tier (16-17μm fiber), but pricing is competitive. The expo is massive (50,000+ booths), so plan to spend 3 days minimum.

**Registration**: Free for international trade buyers. Pre-register via cantonfair.org.cn and bring business cards, passport, and your company import license.

## How to Plan Your Trade Show Visits Around the Buying Cycle

Most brands follow one of three patterns depending on volume and brand maturity:

**High-volume / 3+ years old brands (USD 500K+ annual cashmere purchase)**: Attend 2-3 shows per year. Typically SpinExpo Shanghai (March for SS, September for AW) + Première Vision Paris (September) + Canton Fair (October). Total annual trade show budget: USD 30K-60K including flights, hotels, buyer passes, dinners.

**Mid-volume / 1-3 years old (USD 50K-500K annual)**: Attend 1-2 shows per year. SpinExpo Shanghai is the most efficient — one trip covers yarn sourcing, fabric trends, and lets you visit Ordos mills on the same trip. Budget: USD 8K-15K per trip.

**New brands / low volume (under USD 50K)**: Skip shows for the first year. Use online directories (Alibaba, Made-in-China), request samples from 5-10 factories, and visit one or two factories in person if your order volume justifies it. Trade shows for new brands typically yield conversations but no orders — better to spend it on factory visits once you have specific product specs to discuss.

## What to Bring and How to Maximize Your Time

Practical tips from a decade of trade show attendance:

**Bring**: 200+ business cards (you'll give away 50-100 per day), a notebook for swatch samples with mill codes, your laptop with your fabric/yarn specifications, and a small scale (USD 30 luggage scale) to weigh yarn cones if you're sourcing yarn. Your business registration documents in case a mill asks for them on the spot.

**Don't bring**: Excess expectations. Most trade show meetings yield samples, not orders. The relationship starts at the show and matures over 6-12 months of email communication and trial orders. Don't expect to sign a USD 100K order on the spot unless you've already done 2-3 orders with the mill.

**Plan booth visits in advance**. Use the show's mobile app (all major shows have them) to mark must-see mills. Walk the show floor once without stopping to get the lay of the land, then circle back to priority booths. Most trade show apps let you pre-book meetings with exhibitors.

**Follow up within 48 hours**. Send a short email to every mill you spoke with, summarizing your conversation and next steps. Mills receive 100+ post-show emails; the ones that get a personalized follow-up get a response.

## When Not to Attend a Trade Show

Trade shows are valuable, but not for every situation:

- **You don't have a clear product spec yet**: If you can't describe what you want in 3 sentences (yarn count, micron, gauge, target price), you'll waste the visit. Get the spec clear first.
- **You're still validating the market**: Before committing USD 50K to a cashmere brand, sample 5-10 factories by email and ship 3-5 small orders. The trade show is for scaling, not validating.
- **You're visiting only one or two mills**: If you've already narrowed to 1-2 mills, skip the show and visit the mills directly. More focused time, lower cost.
