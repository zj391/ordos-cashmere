## HS Codes for Cashmere in 2026

HS codes are the backbone of customs clearance. Misclassification can lead to delays, fines, or even seizure. For cashmere imports, the relevant chapters are:

| Product Type | HS Code (2022-2026) | Description | Duty Notes |
|--------------|---------------------|-------------|------------|
| Raw cashmere (dehaired) | 5102.11 | Fine animal hair, cashmere | Duty varies by country; typically 0-5% for raw material |
| Cashmere yarn | 5108.10 | Carded cashmere yarn | Higher duty; check preferential agreements |
| Cashmere fabric | 5111.11 | Woven fabric of cashmere | Duty often 8-12% |
| Cashmere garments | 6110.12 | Knitted sweaters, pullovers | Highest duty; quota restrictions in some markets |

**Key updates for 2026:**
- The EU has tightened origin rules for cashmere garments under the new GSP scheme. Ensure your factory provides a Certificate of Origin (Form A for GSP beneficiaries).
- The US (HTSUS) continues to apply Section 301 tariffs on certain Chinese goods. Check if your cashmere product is exempted (most yarn and fabric are, but garments may not be).
- Japan and South Korea maintain preferential rates under RCEP for cashmere yarn and fabric.

**Pro tip:** Always confirm the exact 10-digit HS code with your customs broker. For example, cashmere sweaters (6110.12.00) versus cashmere cardigans (6110.12.00) may be the same code, but blended fibers (e.g., 70% cashmere, 30% silk) fall under 6110.30.00.

## Customs Clearance Process

Importing cashmere from China involves several steps. Here is the streamlined process we recommend to our clients:

1. **Pre-shipment inspection:** DONGXIAO provides third-party testing (SGS, Bureau Veritas) for fiber content, micron count, and colorfastness. This avoids disputes at destination.
2. **Documentation checklist:**
   - Commercial invoice (with unit price, total value, HS code)
   - Packing list (with net/gross weight, carton dimensions)
   - Bill of lading or airway bill
   - Certificate of origin (Form A or COO)
   - Phytosanitary certificate (for raw cashmere)
   - Test reports (OEKO-TEX, GOTS if applicable)
3. **Customs declaration in China:** The exporter files an e-declaration. DONGXIAO uses the China Customs single window system, ensuring compliance.
4. **Export clearance:** Usually 1-2 days. No export duties for cashmere in 2026.
5. **Destination customs:** Your broker files entry with the local customs authority. Be prepared for potential physical inspection (less than 5% of shipments).
6. **Duty payment and release:** Pay duties and VAT/GST. Release typically within 48 hours.

## 5 Common Mistakes When Importing Cashmere from China

### Mistake 1: Misclassifying the HS Code

**The problem:** Many buyers assume all cashmere products fall under a single code. For example, classifying a cashmere-silk blend scarf as pure cashmere (6110.12) instead of 6110.30 (blended). This triggers audits and penalties.

**How to avoid:** Always provide the exact fiber composition (e.g., 95% cashmere, 5% wool) to your factory. DONGXIAO includes the HS code on every invoice and can advise on the correct classification based on your product.

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Country-Specific Rules of Origin

**The problem:** The EU, US, and Japan have different rules for what qualifies as "originating" cashmere. If your factory uses Chinese cashmere but you want duty-free entry under a Free Trade Agreement, you must meet the regional value content (RVC) or tariff shift requirements.

**How to avoid:** For the EU, cashmere garments must be wholly obtained or undergo sufficient processing (e.g., from yarn to garment). DONGXIAO can provide a cost breakdown to support RVC calculations. Always request a Certificate of Origin from your supplier.

### Mistake 3: Overlooking Quality Consistency

**The problem:** Cashmere quality varies by season and batch. Without proper testing, you might receive fiber that is coarser (e.g., 17 microns instead of 15) or shorter staple length, affecting the final product.

**How to avoid:** Insist on a pre-production sample and a bulk shipment sample. DONGXIAO offers mill-certified testing for micron count, length, and strength. We also recommend third-party lab testing at your cost (typically $200-500 per shipment).

### Mistake 4: Underestimating Lead Times and Seasonality

**The problem:** Cashmere production is seasonal. Raw cashmere is harvested in spring (April-June), and spinning mills are busiest from July to October. If you order in December for January delivery, you may face long delays or pay a premium.

**How to avoid:** Plan 4-6 months ahead. For example, for autumn/winter 2026 collections, place orders by March 2026. DONGXIAO maintains a buffer stock of raw cashmere to shorten lead times, but early booking is always better.

### Mistake 5: Neglecting Sustainability Certifications

**The problem:** Importers increasingly face demands from retailers and consumers for proof of ethical and environmental practices. Without certifications like GOTS, OEKO-TEX, or the Cashmere & Camel Hair Manufacturers Institute (CCMI) certification, you may lose contracts.

**How to avoid:** Choose a factory that holds these certifications. DONGXIAO is ISO9001, OEKO-TEX Standard 100, and GOTS certified. We can also provide a sustainability statement for your brand.
