# How to Source Cashmere Sweaters Wholesale from China: B2B Buyer's FW2025 Decision Guide

Sourcing cashmere knitwear from China is the highest-leverage decision a private-label buyer makes — it determines fiber quality, MOQ flexibility, decoration options, and final retail margin. But the decision is rarely simple. Each sourcing choice (origin, gauge, construction, decoration, certification, shipping) cascades through cost, lead time, and brand positioning.

This guide walks through the 7 critical decisions for a FW2025 private-label cashmere sweater program. The decision tree below is built from 23+ years of Ordos factory experience and reflects the choices that consistently produce successful B2B programs.

## Decision 1: Yarn Origin (Inner Mongolia Cashmere vs Alashan vs Aligned Blends)

The single most important sourcing decision is yarn origin. Inner Mongolia produces approximately 70% of the world's cashmere fiber, and the Ordos / Alashan regions produce the highest-grade Grade A cashmere at 14.5-15.5µm fiber diameter.

| Origin | Fiber Diameter | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Ordos Inner Mongolia | 14.5-15.5µm | Standard premium cashmere |
| Alashan | 14.0-15.0µm | Premium-tier and gift-set programs |
| Iranian | 15.5-17.0µm | Mid-market retail (lower cost) |
| Mongolian (Republic) | 15.0-16.5µm | Mid-to-premium retail |
| Recycled cashmere blends | Mixed | Eco-positioned programs |

**Our recommendation**: For FW2025 private-label collections at the $150-400 retail tier, Alashan or Ordos Grade A cashmere at 14.5-15.5µm is the standard. The fiber diameter directly drives hand-feel and pilling resistance — both of which determine consumer perception of quality.

## Decision 2: Gauge and Yarn Count

Gauge (needles per inch) and yarn count (Nm) together determine fabric weight, drape, and program suitability.

| Gauge | Yarn | Weight Range | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5gg | 2/16 Nm | 480-620g | Outerwear-replacement chunky |
| 7-9gg | 2/26 Nm | 320-480g | Boutique chunky / gift-set |
| 10-12gg | 2/26 Nm | 240-340g | Standard commercial cashmere |
| 12-14gg | 2/48 Nm | 200-280g | Refined office-wear / fine knit |
| 14gg+ | 2/60 Nm | 180-240g | Premium dress / athleisure |

For most FW2025 private-label programs, the **12gg refined tier is the default**. It balances fiber expression, drape, and machine time per piece — the highest volume at our facility.

## Decision 3: Knit Construction (Fully Fashioned vs Cut-and-Sew Knit)

Two construction methods are common for cashmere sweaters:

**Fully fashioned knit**: knit-to-shape panels are linked together at the seams. Standard for premium retail. Better drape, cleaner finishing, slightly higher cost.

**Cut-and-sew knit**: knit fabric is produced as yardage, then cut and assembled like woven garments. More pattern flexibility, slightly lower cost, common for fashion-forward styles with complex silhouettes.

For FW2025 capsule programs at $180-380 retail, fully fashioned knit is the default.

## Decision 6: Certification

| Certification | Cost Premium | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| OEKO-TEX Standard 100 | Included | All programs (default) |
| Sustainable Fibre Alliance (SFA) | +3-5 days lead time | Premium buyers requiring herder-welfare documentation |
| ISO 9001 | Included | All programs |
| GOTS (organic) | +8-15% fiber cost | Eco-positioned programs (organic cashmere is rare) |
| BCI (Better Cotton) | Not applicable | Not relevant for cashmere |

**Recommendation**: OEKO-TEX Standard 100 + SFA chain-of-custody is the standard certification tier for FW2025 B2B programs at $200+ retail.

| Shipping Mode | Lead Time | Cost Per Piece (USD) | Best For |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the typical MOQ for a private-label cashmere sweater program?

MOQ is 100 pieces per design in stock colors. Custom Pantone from 5 kg per color (about 100 sweaters). Custom gauge or yarn count from 200 pieces.

### How long does it take to receive a sample?

7-10 days for sample production, plus 5-7 days for international shipping. Express courier (DHL/FedEx) reduces shipping to 3-5 days.

### What is the most common mistake B2B buyers make?

Specifying a fine-gauge 14gg sweater for a gift-set program. The yarn is too thin to express cashmere's hand-feel benefit. Use 7-12gg for gift-set programs.

### Can I source cashmere and merino wool blend programs from the same factory?

Yes. The same facility can produce 100% cashmere, cashmere-merino blend (typically 70/30 or 80/20), and 100% merino programs. MOQ and lead time are the same across fiber types.

### What is the cost premium for Alashan vs Ordos Inner Mongolia cashmere?

Alashan cashmere at 14.0-15.0µm is typically +10-15% over Ordos Grade A at 14.5-15.5µm. The premium is justified for gift-set and ultra-premium programs.
