# Cashmere Leggings Manufacturing: B2B Buyer's Guide

Cashmere leggings are one of the fastest-growing segments in cashmere apparel, with retail prices of $120–350 per pair. They blend the comfort of loungewear with the luxury of cashmere — a high-margin product for B2B buyers targeting premium athleisure and home-wear markets.

This guide covers manufacturing specifications, MOQ, lead time, and target customer segments from a 23-year Ordos cashmere factory.

## Why Cashmere Leggings Are a Growing Segment

Three market drivers:

**Loungewear boom post-2020**: The home-wear category grew 30%+ during the pandemic and has stabilized at higher levels than pre-2020. Cashmere leggings occupy the premium tier of this category.

**Athleisure crossover**: Premium athleisure brands (Outdoor Voices, Year of Ours) have legitimized leggings as outerwear, not just undergarments. Cashmere elevates the category further.

**Travel wear**: Cashmere leggings are a staple in first-class travel wardrobes — warm, comfortable, refined appearance for airport-to-hotel transitions.

## Fabric Construction Choices

**Plain knit circular**: Most common construction. Soft, drapey, suitable for everyday wear. Best for loungewear and travel wear.

**Plain knit with ribbed waistband**: Adds structure at the waist without bulk. Better for athleisure wear where the leggings need to stay in place during movement.

**Interlock knit**: Denser, more structured fabric. Best for cooler-climate markets where customers want more substantial leggings. Adds 15–20% to fabric weight.

**Jacquard knit**: Patterned fabric (e.g., geometric patterns, brand monograms). Premium positioning but adds USD 3–8 per piece.

## Target Customer Segments

**Premium athleisure brands**: Outerwear-grade leggings with cashmere content (70% cashmere / 30% merino is the sweet spot for this segment). MOQ 200–500 pieces per SKU.

**Loungewear and home-wear brands**: 100% cashmere leggings for the home-wear customer who wants maximum softness. MOQ 100–200 pieces per SKU.

**Travel wear brands**: Lightweight cashmere leggings (180–220g) for first-class travel wardrobes. Often sold in sets with matching eye mask and socks.

**Gift retailers**: Cashmere leggings as standalone luxury gifts or as part of a curated cashmere wardrobe set (top + legging).

## Common Mistakes B2B Buyers Make

**Mistake 1: Choosing too heavy a fabric for loungewear.** A 380g legging is wonderful for cold-climate outdoor wear but feels heavy for home loungewear. Most loungewear customers prefer 200–280g.

**Mistake 2: Skipping the elastic waistband reinforcement.** Standard elastic loses 30–40% of its stretch in 6 months. Reinforced elastic with hidden band maintains stretch for 18–24 months.

**Mistake 3: Not specifying inseam length.** Default 76 cm inseam fits most heights but excludes tall (need 80+ cm) and petite (need 70 cm). Custom inseam from 300 pieces per size run.

**Mistake 4: Underestimating the importance of color fastness.** Cashmere leggings in dark colors (black, navy, charcoal) can bleed onto furniture and other garments if not properly dyed. Specify color fastness requirements (4–5 on the gray scale) in the inquiry.

## Sustainability Considerations

Cashmere leggings customers often expect:
- OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification
- Sustainable Fibre Alliance (SFA) certification
- Cashmere-merino blends (50/50 or 70/30) for durability and lower cost
- Recycled cashmere blends (70% virgin / 30% recycled)
- Carbon-neutral shipping options

These add USD 0.50–3.00 per piece but enable premium positioning in the conscious consumer market.
