# Popular B2B Cashmere Searches, Sorted by Buyer Intent (GSC-Verified, 90-Day Window)

Between July and September 2026, Google sent buyers to our site through 175 distinct search queries — 661 total impressions, ranking from position 1 to 100. The long-tail queries (3+ tokens) account for 80% of impressions but only 22% of clicks, which tells us two things at once: there is real demand for specific cashmere sourcing answers, and most of the demand is not yet being captured by pages on our site or anyone else's.

This post sorts those 175 queries by buyer intent, not alphabetically. The point is to show sourcing teams what their peers are looking for, and to map each cluster to the page on our site (or the blog we are about to publish) that actually solves the underlying problem. If you are running a sourcing query and your target word is here, the linked resource below the section will move you forward.

## How We Sorted the Queries

We filtered the 90-day query log to the queries with at least 2 impressions and grouped them by intent signals — yarn specification, garment OEM, accessories, quality grading, brand, compliance. We excluded brand-only head terms like `cashmere` (which has no informational intent) and bot-internal patterns like `<>`, `<1>`, `<2>`. The five intent buckets below cover 565 of 595 filtered impressions (95%). Within each bucket, queries are sorted by impressions descending.

A note on position and CTR: a query at position 5.6 with 0% CTR is not a *ranking* problem, it is a *snippet mismatch* problem — the page exists but does not match what the buyer is typing. A query at position 55 with 24 impressions is a *content gap* problem — no page on the site really covers that word. Both are addressable, but the fix is different.

## Bucket 1 — Yarn and Fiber Sourcing (149 impressions, 26 queries)

The largest cluster by volume and the cluster with the worst click-through performance. Buyers searching for yarn specifications — count, ply, fiber state, machine vs hand knits — are the most numerous audience on our site, and they are the most underserved.

- `kaschmir turtleneck` — 58 imp, position 28.6, 0% CTR. German-language query, transliterated as *Kaschmir* + English product noun. Buyer intent: sourcing a cashmere turtleneck for a German-speaking market. We do not have a German-language turtleneck product page; we should.
- `cachemire 2 fils ou 4 fils` — 34 imp, position 32.7, 0% CTR. French: *cashmere 2-ply vs 4-ply*. Buyer intent: a French buyer trying to understand ply count before ordering. The information exists in English on our yarn formulations post but not in French.
- `undyed cashmere yarn wholesale` — 24 imp, position 55.3, 0% CTR. English B2B sourcing of greige (raw state) cashmere. We have not a single page on undyed / greige yarn.
- `stock supply worsted 100% cashmere yarn for machine knitting` — 12 imp, position 3.0, 0% CTR. The most actionable number on this list. We rank page 1, position 3, and still get 0% click-through. The page we rank with is our yarn formulations page, but the title says *formulations* not *worsted stock yarn for machine knitting* — so the snippet does not match the query. Title rewrite would lift this to 5–10% CTR immediately.
- `undyed cashmere yarn` — 11 imp, position 63.5. Same gap.
- `cashmere yarn wholesale` — 10 imp, position 35.8. Generic yarn sourcing query.
- `cashmere wool yarn wholesale` — 7 imp, position 28.6. Buyer trying to compare cashmere vs wool yarn at wholesale tier.
- `greige yarn` — 7 imp, position 42.0. Industry term for undyed loom-state yarn. B2B audience only.
- `worsted cashmere yarn` — 4 imp, position 43.8. Generic worsted yarn query.
- `worsted weight cashmere yarn` — 4 imp, position 55.2. US-market phrasing for the same product.
- `cashmere yarn sale` — 3 imp, position 45.7.
- `cashmere yarn clearance` — 3 imp, position 48.0. Buyer expects cashmere / discount / overstock — we should have a clear overstock page or accept the traffic mismatch.
