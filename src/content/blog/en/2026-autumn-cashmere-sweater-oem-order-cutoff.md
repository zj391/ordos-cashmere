## Why Cutoff Dates Exist in Cashmere OEM

Unlike polyester or cotton knitwear, cashmere production is constrained by a hard biological clock. The dehairing season in Inner Mongolia runs from April to June; by September, the raw material for the year is already allocated to yarn spinners and we are deep into the dyeing-spinning-knitting pipeline. Once our knitting lines, dyeing vats, and finishing capacity are booked, adding more orders means displacing existing customers — which is something we will not do.

This is why every autumn we publish cutoff dates publicly. Buyers who book before the cutoff get:

- **Confirmed production slot** on the knitting line.
- **Yarn allocation** from our 2026 dehairing pool.
- **Realistic shipping windows** to EU/US/JP ports.
- **No expedite surcharge** (late orders after the cutoff face 15-25% expedite fees, if capacity exists at all).

Buyers who book after the cutoff either pay a premium for line re-scheduling, simplify the garment to fit a shorter window, or wait for Q1 2027 production.

## What Happens If You Miss the Cutoff

Three options, in order of cost:

### Option 1: Expedite with Surcharge (Capacity Available)

If we have a partial gap on the knitting line — for example, a buyer cancelled or downsized — we can slot in late orders with a **15-25% expedite surcharge**. This surcharge covers overtime labor, shifted yarn allocation, and the risk premium. Typical expedite windows:

- 1-2 weeks past cutoff: 15% surcharge, ex-works delayed by 7-10 days.
- 3-4 weeks past cutoff: 20-25% surcharge, ex-works delayed by 14-21 days.

Availability is by inquiry only. Do not assume capacity exists.

### Option 2: Simplify the Garment

Strip out the bottleneck step. Common simplifications:

- **Custom yarn → stock yarn**: saves 2-3 weeks. You accept our standard color card (currently 42 stock colors).
- **Complex stitch → plain knit**: saves 1-2 weeks. Loses design differentiation but gains speed.
- **Embroidery/print after knitting instead of in-knit**: saves 1 week. Acceptable for chest logos, not allover patterns.
- **Custom labels → stock labels with sticker overlay**: saves 3-5 days. Common for sub-300 pc trial orders.

### Option 3: Move to Q1 2027 Production

If the order cannot realistically hit Q4 2026, we will quote for **Q1 2027 production** (ex-works January-February 2027). This is the default for new design development and orders that need full custom yarn + complex stitch + custom trims. Q1 2027 slots open for booking on **2026-10-15**.
