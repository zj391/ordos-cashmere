# Cashmere Travel Wrap: B2B Buyer's Guide to Manufacturing Luxury Travel Sets

Cashmere travel wraps are a fast-growing segment in luxury travel retail, with first-class airlines, premium hotels, and resort gift shops selling these as both functional accessories and high-margin gifts. A 200×100 cm cashmere travel wrap with matching eye mask and socks sells for $250–500 as a bundle.

For B2B buyers entering this segment, the manufacturing specs differ from standard cashmere scarves and shawls. This guide covers what you need to know from a 23-year Ordos cashmere factory.

## What Is a Cashmere Travel Wrap?

A cashmere travel wrap is a 200×100 cm (or larger) cashmere fabric designed to drape over the shoulders or wrap around the body during travel. It serves as:
- **In-flight or in-train warmth** — first-class passengers use it instead of airline blankets
- **Resort cover-up** — for women who want a lightweight wrap over evening wear
- **Loungewear layering** — for hotel room wear
- **Gift item** — bundled with eye mask and socks as a curated travel set

The fabric must drape softly without bulk, feel comfortable against bare skin, and pack small enough to fit in a carry-on.

## Target Markets for Cashmere Travel Sets

**First-class airline partnerships**: Airlines like Emirates, Singapore Airlines, Cathay Pacific offer amenity kits and travel wraps as part of premium cabin service. Order volumes typically 5,000–20,000 sets per year per airline. Custom branding on the wrap and packaging is standard.

**Premium hotel chains**: Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton, Aman, Mandarin Oriental place cashmere travel wraps in premium suites. Order volumes 1,000–10,000 sets per year. Custom embroidery on the wrap corner is common.

**Resort gift shops and luxury retail**: Curated travel sets are sold as gift items. Order volumes 100–500 sets per SKU. Custom packaging and brand storytelling drive higher margins.

**Corporate gifting**: Premium corporate gifts for executives and clients. Order volumes 50–500 sets per event. Custom packaging with brand logo is the highest-margin option.

## Customization Options

**Custom packaging**: Branded boxes (rigid or folding), branded dust bags, branded tissue, custom hangtags. Minimum order varies by component but typically 100 sets for branded boxes.

**Embroidered logo**: Wrap corner or center, eye mask front, sock cuff. From 200 sets. Embroidery preserves the cashmere hand-feel better than printed labels.

**Woven label**: Sewn into the wrap seam, eye mask interior, sock interior. From 100 sets. Most common for established brands.

**Custom Pantone**: Across wrap, eye mask, and socks. Lab dip in 5–7 days, bulk production 25–30 days after approval.

**Custom gift box inserts**: Branded cards, branded care instructions, custom ribbon. From 500 sets.

## Common Mistakes B2B Buyers Make

**Mistake 1: Ordering a heavy wrap for tropical climates.** A 280g wrap is wonderful in Nordic winters and unbearable in Singapore. For tropical travel markets, specify 200g or lighter.

**Mistake 2: Skipping the satin lining on the eye mask.** Cashmere against the eye area can feel scratchy on sensitive skin. A silk or satin lining makes the eye mask comfortable for actual sleep use.

**Mistake 3: Not bundling care instructions.** Cashmere travel sets are bought as gifts, often by people unfamiliar with cashmere care. A small branded care card with the wash instructions prevents damage and complaints.

**Mistake 4: Underestimating packaging costs.** Custom rigid gift boxes cost USD 4–8 per set at 100-piece MOQ. Budget for packaging from the start — it can double the unit cost.

## Sustainability Considerations

The luxury travel segment increasingly requires:
- OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification
- Sustainable Fibre Alliance (SFA) certification for herder welfare
- Recycled cashmere blends (70% virgin / 30% recycled)
- FSC-certified packaging
- Carbon-neutral shipping options

These add USD 0.50–3.00 per piece but enable premium positioning in the luxury travel market.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the typical MOQ for a cashmere travel set?

MOQ is 100 sets per design. Custom Pantone across all three components (wrap + eye mask + socks) requires 5 kg per color minimum. Custom packaging typically requires 100 sets minimum.

### What yarn weight should I specify for the wrap?

2/26 Nm woolen-spun for the standard 240–280g wrap. 2/48 Nm worsted-spun for a refined, dressier 220g wrap. 2/60 Nm worsted-spun for ultra-lightweight 180g wraps for tropical travel markets.

### Can I get custom packaging for hotel or airline branding?

Yes. Branded rigid gift boxes from 100 sets. Branded dust bags from 500 sets. Custom ribbon and tissue from 100 sets. Most premium hotel and airline partnerships include fully custom packaging.

### What is the lead time for a 500-set custom Pantone order?

Approximately 35–45 days total: 5–7 days for lab dip, 25–30 days bulk production, 3–5 days QC, plus 20–35 days sea freight (depending on destination). Air freight reduces transit to 5–7 days if faster delivery is required.
