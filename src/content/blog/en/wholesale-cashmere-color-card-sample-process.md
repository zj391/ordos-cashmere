### Why a Color Card Matters for Wholesale Cashmere

A cashmere color card is a swatch book or fan deck that displays the available dye colors for cashmere yarn, fabric, or finished garments. For a B2B buyer, it serves three purposes:

- **Color accuracy**: Digital screens are notoriously unreliable for matching shades. A physical card ensures your team and our production team see the same color.
- **Material quality**: You can feel the hand-feel, thickness, and fiber quality—something no PDF can replicate.
- **Production planning**: Color cards often indicate which shades are standard (low MOQ) versus custom (higher MOQ).

At DONGXIAO, our color cards are built on decades of experience dyeing cashmere in Ordos, where the finest Inner Mongolian fibers are processed. Every card includes 40–60 standard shades, plus options for custom Pantone matching.

### What to Send in Your B2B Color Card Request

To get the right color card and samples quickly, your initial request should be specific. Here’s a checklist of what to include:

1. **Your company profile** – Name, country, and business type (importer, brand, trading company). This helps us tailor the response.
2. **Product category** – Are you sourcing raw cashmere fiber, yarn, fabric, or finished garments? Each has a different color card format.
3. **Target fiber specifications** – If known, specify micron count (e.g., 15.5–16.5 microns), yarn count (e.g., 2/28 NM), or fabric weight (e.g., 220 GSM). This narrows down the card.
4. **Quantity estimates** – Your estimated annual or per-order volume. This informs MOQ discussions and whether we can send a full card or a smaller set.
5. **Color preferences** – If you have a Pantone reference or a brand palette, mention it. We can highlight matching shades on the card.
6. **Shipping address** – Physical address for courier delivery. Note that we ship worldwide via DHL, FedEx, or UPS.
7. **Timeline expectations** – When do you need the card? This helps us prioritize.

**Example inquiry**:

> Subject: Color Card Request for 2/28 NM Cashmere Yarn
> 
> Hi, we are a knitwear brand in Japan importing 5,000 kg of cashmere yarn annually. We need a color card for 2/28 NM, 15.5–16.5 micron yarn. Please send standard shades and advise if you can match Pantone 19-4020. Our address is [Tokyo, Japan]. We need this by next month.

### Factors That Affect Sample Speed

Several variables can speed up or delay your sample request:

- **Stock availability**: Standard colors are always in stock. Custom colors require dyeing, which takes longer.
- **Fiber specifications**: If you request a non-standard micron count (e.g., 14.5 microns), we may need to source special lots, adding 1–2 weeks.
- **Quantity of samples**: Requesting 20 swatches vs. 5 swatches affects preparation time.
- **Seasonality**: Our factory runs production year-round, but sample requests spike before trade shows (e.g., Pitti Filati, Première Vision). Plan ahead.
- **Customs and shipping**: International shipping is usually reliable, but some countries have stricter customs inspections. We recommend using express couriers to minimize delays.

### How to Get Color-Matched Samples (Custom Development)

If your brand requires exact Pantone or brand-specific colors, here’s the process:

1. **Send your color reference** – Provide Pantone codes, hex codes, or physical swatches.
2. **Lab dip** – We dye small yarn/fabric samples to match your reference. This takes 5–7 business days.
3. **Approval** – You review lab dips under daylight and artificial light. We may adjust the formula (1–2 rounds).
4. **Production sample** – Once approved, we create a full-size sample for your bulk order.

**Cost**: Lab dips are typically $50–$100 per color, but at DONGXIAO, we waive this fee for orders above 500 kg per color. All custom sample costs are deducted from your first production invoice.

### What to Do After Receiving Your Color Card

Once your card arrives, don’t just file it away. Use it strategically:

- **Evaluate hand-feel**: Compare the swatch against your quality standards. Cashmere should feel soft, not prickly.
- **Test color fastness**: Rub the swatch on a white cloth to check for dye transfer. High-quality dyeing (as per OEKO-TEX standards) should not bleed.
- **Check fiber content**: Ask for a certificate of analysis to confirm 100% cashmere (or your specified blend).
- **Plan your collection**: Use the card to shortlist colors that align with your market trends.

If you need additional swatches for your design team, just ask—we can send duplicate cards at cost.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid in B2B Sample Requests

To ensure a smooth process, steer clear of these pitfalls:

- **Vague requests**: “Send me your color card” without context leads to generic responses. Specify your product category.
- **Ignoring MOQ**: Some buyers request custom colors without realizing the MOQ (usually 200–300 kg per color for yarn). Ask upfront.
- **Assuming instant turnaround**: Sampling takes time. Plan 2–4 weeks ahead of your production schedule.
- **Not checking shipping costs**: International shipping can be $50–$100. Confirm who bears the cost.
- **Overlooking certifications**: If you need GOTS or OEKO-TEX documentation, request it during sampling, not after production starts.
