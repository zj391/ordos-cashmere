# Cashmere Hijab Manufacturing: B2B Guide to MOQ, Fabric Weight, and Lead Time

The modest fashion market is one of the fastest-growing segments in luxury apparel, with cashmere hijabs commanding premium retail prices ($80–300 per piece). For B2B buyers entering this segment, the manufacturing specs differ from standard cashmere scarves — finer yarn, lighter fabric weight, and longer standard lengths.

This guide covers what cashmere hijab buyers need to know from a 23-year Ordos cashmere factory.

## Why Cashmere Hijabs Are Different from Cashmere Scarves

A cashmere hijab is not a narrow scarf. Three structural differences:

**Length**: Standard cashmere hijabs run 180–200 cm long, similar to scarves but with longer drape requirements.

**Width**: Standard cashmere hijabs run 50–70 cm wide, narrower than scarves (35–45 cm) and pashmina shawls (70–100 cm). The narrower width is because hijabs wrap once around the head and frame the face — too wide creates bulk.

**Weight**: 80–140 g per piece for standard cashmere hijabs, lighter than scarves (60–120 g) but heavier than ultra-fine silk blends.

**Fabric construction**: Plain weave (1/1) or twill on rapier looms. The fabric must drape softly without bulk — a heavy hand-feel defeats the purpose of cashmere in hijab form.

**MOQ**: 100 pcs per design in stock colors. Custom Pantone from 5 kg per color. Custom dimensions from 100 pcs.

**Lead time**: 25–30 days bulk production + 3–5 days QC + 20–35 days sea freight to EU/US/Middle East. Air freight 5–7 days if faster delivery is needed (adds USD 1.50–3.00 per kg).

**Sample timeline**: 5–7 days for sample sets at USD 60–120 per piece, refundable against first bulk order.

**Embroidered logo**: From 200 pcs, in any location (corner, end edge, center back). Embroidery is the most popular customization for hijab brands because it preserves the fabric hand-feel.

**Woven label**: From 100 pcs, sewn into the end edge. Most common for established modest fashion brands.

## Common Mistakes B2B Buyers Make

**Mistake 1: Ordering standard scarf dimensions for hijab.** Scarf dimensions are 180×35–45 cm; hijab dimensions are 180×50–70 cm. A scarf sold as a hijab will be too narrow for proper wrapping.

**Mistake 2: Choosing too heavy a yarn.** Cashmere hijab should feel light and airy. 2/26 Nm yarn is too heavy for hijab — the wrap will feel bulky on the head. Use 2/48 Nm or finer.

**Mistake 3: Skipping the hand-rolled edge.** Machine-hemmed edges can itch against the face. Hand-rolled edges are essential for hijab — the fabric must feel soft against skin and hair.

**Mistake 4: Not specifying the modest fashion market in the inquiry.** Cashmere factories typically default to fashion scarf or shawl specs. Buyers should specify "modest fashion hijab" or "hijab" explicitly to get the correct dimensions, fabric weight, and edge finishing.

## Sustainability Considerations

The modest fashion market is increasingly sustainability-focused. Cashmere buyers in this segment often request:
- OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification (no harmful substances)
- Sustainable Fibre Alliance (SFA) certification for herder welfare
- Recycled cashmere blends (70% virgin / 30% recycled)
- Carbon-neutral shipping options

These add USD 0.50–3.00 per piece but enable premium positioning in the modest fashion market.
