# How to Choose Cashmere Scarf Weight (GSM) by Climate

Cashmere scarf weight is the single biggest factor in customer satisfaction — and the most common source of returns. A 60g lightweight cashmere scarf is perfect for tropical climates and feels suffocating in arctic winters; a 240g heavyweight cashmere scarf is perfect for Nordic winters and feels unbearable in Mediterranean autumn.

B2B buyers who don't match weight to target climate end up with high return rates and poor reviews. This guide breaks down cashmere scarf weights, GSM ranges, and which weight works best for which climate.

## Cashmere Scarf Weight Categories

Cashmere scarves are typically classified by finished weight per piece:

| Category | Weight Range | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Ultra-lightweight | 40–70 g | Tropical, sub-tropical, indoor use |
| Lightweight | 70–120 g | Mediterranean, mild autumn/spring |
| Medium | 120–180 g | Most European/US climates |
| Heavyweight | 180–280 g | Nordic, Russian, Canadian, Midwest US winters |

## GSM (Grams per Square Meter) Reference

GSM is the standard textile measurement of fabric density. Cashmere scarves range from 80 GSM (very sheer) to 380 GSM (very dense):

| GSM | Hand-Feel | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| 80–120 GSM | Air-light, almost translucent | Tropical climates |
| 120–180 GSM | Soft, light, drapey | Mediterranean autumn/spring |
| 180–250 GSM | Substantial, warm | Most European/US winters |
| 250–350 GSM | Heavy, structured | Nordic/Russian winters |
| 350+ GSM | Very heavy, blanket-like | Extreme cold |

## Climate-Matching Guide

### Tropical Climates (Singapore, UAE, Hong Kong, Caribbean)
**Recommended weight**: 50–90 g per piece (80–140 GSM)
**Recommended yarn**: 2/60 Nm or finer (2/80 Nm for ultra-luxury)
**Recommended weave**: Plain weave, lightweight
**Best fabric blends**: 100% cashmere, or cashmere-silk blends (70/30) for the lightest drape
**Retail price range**: $80–250 per piece
**Customer complaint if wrong weight**: "Too warm," "Too heavy," "Feels suffocating"

### Mediterranean Climates (Spain, Italy, Greece, Southern France, California)
**Recommended weight**: 70–130 g per piece (130–200 GSM)
**Recommended yarn**: 2/48 Nm worsted-spun
**Recommended weave**: Plain weave or twill
**Retail price range**: $80–220 per piece
**Customer complaint if wrong weight**: "Too heavy for the season"

### Northern European Climates (UK, Germany, Netherlands, Northern France, Northeast US)
**Recommended weight**: 120–200 g per piece (200–280 GSM)
**Recommended yarn**: 2/26 to 2/48 Nm (worsted or woolen-spun)
**Recommended weave**: Plain weave, twill, or jacquard
**Retail price range**: $100–300 per piece
**Customer complaint if wrong weight**: "Too thin for real cold," "Doesn't keep me warm"

### Nordic/Russian/Canadian Winters (Norway, Sweden, Finland, Russia, Canada)
**Recommended weight**: 200–280 g per piece (280–380 GSM)
**Recommended yarn**: 2/26 Nm woolen-spun for maximum loft and warmth
**Recommended weave**: Twill or heavy plain weave
**Best fabric blends**: 100% cashmere, or cashmere-merino blends (50/50) for extra warmth
**Retail price range**: $150–400 per piece
**Customer complaint if wrong weight**: "Not warm enough," "Cold in winter"

## Common Mistakes B2B Buyers Make

**Mistake 1: Ordering one universal weight for all markets.** This is the #1 mistake. A 90g scarf works for Singapore but not for Sweden. Match weight to target climate.

**Mistake 2: Confusing yarn count with fabric weight.** 2/26 Nm and 2/48 Nm are yarn specifications; the fabric weight (GSM) depends on the yarn, the weave density, and the finishing. A loosely-woven 2/26 Nm fabric can be lighter than a tightly-woven 2/48 Nm fabric.

**Mistake 3: Ordering based on price alone.** The cheapest cashmere scarves are typically the lightest weight. For cold-climate customers, this creates return rates of 15–25%.

**Mistake 4: Not specifying GSM in the inquiry.** Cashmere factories can produce the same scarf at multiple GSMs. Always specify target weight per piece and target GSM when asking for quotes.

## How to Specify Cashmere Scarf Weight in Your Inquiry

A clear inquiry includes:
- Target weight per piece (e.g., "120g per piece")
- Target GSM (e.g., "200 GSM")
- Target dimensions (e.g., "180×35 cm")
- Target yarn count (e.g., "2/48 Nm worsted-spun")
- Target weave (e.g., "plain weave")
- Target hand-feel (e.g., "soft but substantial")

This avoids ambiguous quotes where one factory's "180g scarf" is another factory's "120g scarf."

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What weight cashmere scarf is best for the US market?

The US has multiple climate zones. Northeast US (Boston, NYC, Chicago) prefers 120–200g medium weight. Southeast US (Florida, Texas) prefers 70–120g lightweight. Pacific Northwest prefers 130–180g medium. California is split — coastal cities prefer 70–120g, mountain areas prefer 130–180g.

### What is the lightest cashmere scarf you can make?

Approximately 40g per piece for an ultra-fine 2/80 Nm cashmere scarf at 180×35 cm. Below this, the fabric becomes too sheer for practical use. Most "lightweight cashmere scarves" on the market run 60–90g.

### Does a heavier cashmere scarf last longer?

Generally yes — heavier scarves use more yarn per square inch and resist wear better. A 200g scarf typically lasts 8–12 years of regular wear; a 90g scarf lasts 5–8 years. For customers in cold climates who want a long-term investment piece, heavier is better.

### Can I get the same scarf in multiple weights?

Yes, from 100 pcs per design per weight. The factory can produce 50g, 90g, 130g, and 200g versions of the same scarf design, using different yarn counts and weave densities. This lets you carry the same brand across multiple climate markets.
